Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does ceramic coating last?

A professionally applied coating typically lasts 2–5 years depending on the product tier and how the car is maintained, with routine washing extending its lifespan.

How long should I book off for a full detail?

Full interior and exterior detailing usually takes 4–8 hours. Paint correction and coating packages run 1–3 days depending on the vehicle's condition.

Do you work on leased or pre-sale vehicles?

Yes — paint correction and detailing are popular ahead of lease returns or private sales, and can noticeably improve resale value and photos.

Do I need to bring my car in, or do you travel?

Most work is done in our Tallinn bay for quality control, but mobile visits are available for full detailing packages within the city — ask when you book.
